PRIVACYPOLICY

Last updated [launch date]

The short version

  • Majority Rules never asks for your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • There are no ads, no ad trackers and no analytics companies in the app.
  • We don't sell your information, and we don't use it to track you across other apps or websites.
  • Worldwide results are only ever shown as percentages. Nobody outside your room sees how you voted.
  • You can delete your data at any time with Delete My Data in Settings.

Who we are

Majority Rules is made by Vincent Ruggieri. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Majority Rules, and "you" means anyone who uses the app.

What we collect

Things you choose to give us

  • Your nickname, your circle (a color or one of our pictures) and your gender (Male, Female or Prefer not to say). Gender is used only to show the Men vs. Women results. You can change any of these in the app.
  • Your votes on the cards you play.
  • Card ideas you send with Suggest A Card!

Things collected automatically

  • A random ID for your phone. The app creates it the first time it opens. It isn't your Apple Account, your name or your phone's advertising ID.
  • Technical details when the app connects to our server: your phone's internet (IP) address and the type of app and phone software making the connection. Our database provider records these with your phone's sign-in, and we clear them after 7 days.
  • Game details: the rooms you create or join, when you were active in them, and whether your phone is in the game or has stepped away.
  • A security check from Apple (App Attest) that your phone is running the real Majority Rules app. It's a code made by your phone's security chip, and it contains nothing about you.
  • If a phone tries to tamper with the game: a record of the attempt, and that phone is blocked.

Things we get from Apple

  • Which Party Packs you bought, as a pack name and Apple's purchase number, so your packs unlock for you and your room. Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details.

What we don't collect

Your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, your phone's advertising ID, or your payment details. We never ask for your email address. If you choose to email us, we'll receive your address and message and use them only to answer you. We never add them to a mailing list, share them, or connect them to your activity in the app.

How your votes are stored

  • For most cards, your vote is saved in the worldwide results under a scrambled code made from your phone's random ID with a secret key. The scrambled code can't be turned back into your ID; it only lets us count your vote once per card (playing a card again updates your vote).
  • For cards that could reveal something personal (every Spicy and R-rated card, the Political Debate and Philosophical & Deep Talks packs, and a few others), your vote is only added to the totals. Nothing about it is saved with any ID at all.

How we use it

  • To run the game: rooms, voting, results, and showing your nickname and circle to the people in your room.
  • To show the worldwide results on each card, overall and for Men vs. Women.
  • To unlock the Party Packs you bought.
  • To review card ideas and add the best ones to the game.
  • To keep the game safe and working: stopping spam and abuse, and fixing problems.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it, rent it or trade it.

What other players see

People in your room see your nickname, your circle and how you voted on each card in that room. Everyone else only ever sees percentages, never who voted.

Who we share it with

  • Our database provider, Supabase (servers in the United States), which stores the game's data for us and may only use it to provide that service.
  • Apple, which handles purchases, delivers the app and runs the App Attest check. If you've turned on "Share With App Developers" in your iPhone's Privacy settings, Apple also sends us anonymous crash reports, which we use only to fix bugs.
  • If the law requires it, for example a valid court order, or to protect people's safety.
  • If Majority Rules is ever sold or merged, the new owner would have to keep following this policy or tell you about any change first.

That's the full list, and anyone who helps us run the app must protect your information at least as well as this policy does. There are no advertising networks, data brokers or analytics companies.

Tracking and privacy signals

We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't "sell" or "share" personal information as California and other US state laws define those words. So there's nothing to opt out of, but we respect privacy signals such as Global Privacy Control anyway.

How long we keep it

  • Rooms (the room code, who joined and the votes in that room) are deleted one day after the room closes.
  • Your scrambled votes and the totals stay in the worldwide results, so the percentages remain accurate.
  • Card ideas stay until we review them. Ideas we don't use are deleted 30 days after we turn them down. Ideas we add become part of the game.
  • Records of blocked spam are deleted after 90 days.
  • Wrong room codes a phone types are counted to stop anyone guessing codes, and the count is deleted within a day.
  • Records of tamper attempts are deleted after 30 days. A phone blocked for tampering stays blocked until it's gone 12 months without opening the app; then everything tied to it is erased.
  • The IP address and app type recorded with your sign-in are cleared after 7 days.
  • If your phone doesn't open the app for 12 months, we delete everything tied to it, just like Delete My Data.
  • Purchase records are kept for as long as you own the packs, so they keep working. If Apple refunds a pack, its record is deleted. The App Attest check is kept until everything tied to your phone is deleted.
  • On your phone, the app remembers your nickname, circle, gender, settings and saved games. Deleting the app removes them.

How we protect it

Everything the app sends travels over an encrypted connection. Our database only lets each phone change its own information, and only our administrator can review card ideas, protected by a two-step sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information, and if a breach ever affected it, we would tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete: tap Delete My Data in Settings. Everything tied to your phone is erased right away, and always within 30 days, except where a law requires us to keep something. Your past votes stay in the world percentages. They’re saved under a scrambled code, not your phone’s ID, and once that ID is erased, there’s nothing left that can link the votes to you, not even for us.
  • Correct: change your nickname, circle and gender in the app at any time.
  • See or get a copy: email us and we'll answer within 30 days (45 days where a law allows it).
  • Permissions and consent: the app doesn't ask for any iPhone permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or notifications). To stop sharing your gender, choose Prefer not to say in Settings at any time.

We'll never treat you differently for using these rights.

California and other US states with privacy laws: you have these rights under those laws too, and you can use an authorized agent. In the last 12 months we collected identifiers (the random ID and IP address), characteristics you chose to share (gender), commercial information (Party Pack purchases) and app activity (votes, rooms and card ideas), only for the purposes above, and we did not sell or share them.

United Kingdom and Switzerland: you can also object to or restrict how we use your information, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner. We use your information because it's needed to provide the game you asked for, because of our legitimate interest in keeping the game safe and improving it, and, for gender, because you chose to give it. Your information is stored in the United States, protected by our agreement with our database provider.

European Union: Majority Rules isn't currently offered in the European Union.

Age

Majority Rules is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and if we learn that we have, we'll delete it. If you believe a minor is using the app, please email us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll also let you know in the app before it takes effect.
