SUPPORT

How many people can play?

One phone can host a room for up to 50 players. Everyone else joins on their own iPhone, iPad or Android phone (iPhones and Androids play in the same room) by typing the room code. Just two of you? Tap 2 Player: you can play on one phone, taking turns, or on two.

Contact us

Email majorityrules.support@protonmail.com with any question, problem or idea. We read every email.

How do I show the game on my TV?

  • Apple TV or an AirPlay TV: join the same Wi-Fi as the TV, swipe down from the top-right corner of your screen to open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and pick your TV. If a code shows on the TV, type it in.
  • Fire TV, Chromecast and some older TVs: search the TV's app store for an AirPlay receiver app, open it, then follow the steps above.
  • No Wi-Fi? An HDMI adapter and cable always work.

On Android:

  • Chromecast, Google TV and most smart TVs: join the same Wi-Fi as the TV, swipe down from the top of your screen twice to open Quick Settings, tap Cast (or Screen cast) and pick your TV. If your phone asks, tap Start now.
  • Samsung, Fire TV and other TVs: turn on Screen Mirroring in the TV's settings, then tap Smart View (or Cast) in Quick Settings and pick your TV. Fire TV and some other TVs call it Miracast.
  • No Wi-Fi? A USB-C to HDMI cable works on most phones. Not every phone can send video out of its USB-C port, so check yours first.

In the game, the TV button in the lobby shows these steps too. While the host's phone is on the TV, everyone else's music turns off by itself, since the TV plays it.

I can't join a room

  • Check the room code with the host. Codes are 6 characters. The host and everyone in the game can see it in Settings.
  • The room may have ended, or be full.
  • If the app says "Too many tries," wait a few minutes and try again.
  • If the app says there's no internet connection, connect to Wi-Fi or turn on cellular data.
  • If the app says the servers are busy, try again in a few minutes. Once you're in a room, you stay in.

How do I turn the music down or off?

Open Settings. Lobby Music and In-Game Music each have their own switch and volume.

My Party Packs are missing

Open Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought them with (on Android, the same Google account in Google Play). Packs bought by anyone in your room unlock for the whole room while they're in it.

Refunds

Party Packs are bought through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple refunds a pack, it locks again.

On Android, packs are bought through Google Play, so Google Play handles refunds. Request one at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory. If Google Play refunds a pack, it locks again.

Suggest a card

Open Settings → Suggest A Card! We review every idea, and the best ones are added to the game.

Delete your data

Open Settings → Delete My Data. Everything tied to your phone is erased right away. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
